About

OperatorCRX / 01

I’m Joseph Mario Danubio, a Computer Science – Software Engineering student at BINUS in Bekasi. I also go by Creax.

I build tools that make complicated things easier to read: a converter that shows what a format change will cost, an advisor dashboard that turns graduation rules into a ranked to-do list, and a hoax checker that keeps its evidence visible. I also design interfaces in Figma and run a small home lab on Proxmox VE, Nginx, MariaDB, and Cloudflare.

Most of my work sits between a messy input and a clear decision. I care about hierarchy, spacing, and navigation that make an interface legible without explanation, and I like software that tells you what it is doing. I work in teams and stay open to feedback.
